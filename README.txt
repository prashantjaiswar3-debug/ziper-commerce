ZIPER E-COMMERCE FRONT-END PREVIEW
Open index.html in a browser, or deploy the complete folder to static hosting.

Included: responsive storefront, category filters, sort, search, product detail modal, wishlist toggle, shopping bag with quantity controls, and demo checkout flow.

IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH:
- Replace sample product names, prices, descriptions, photos, sizes and stock with verified business information.
- Replace hello@ziper.in with your actual support email.
- Connect a secure backend/database for products, customers, inventory and orders.
- Integrate a payment gateway such as Razorpay using server-side order creation and verified webhooks. Do not put secret keys in front-end files.
- Add shipping, return, privacy, terms and contact details.
The checkout is a UI demonstration only: it does not send/store customer information or collect payments.
